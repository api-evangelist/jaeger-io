by Yuri Shkuro and Jonah Kowall Jaeger, the popular open-source distributed tracing system, is getting a major upgrade with the upcoming release of Jaeger v2 . This new version is a new architecture for Jaeger backend components that utilizes OpenTelemetry Collector framework as the base and extends it with Jaeger’s unique features. It promises to bring significant improvements and changes, making Jaeger more flexible, extensible, and even better aligned with the OpenTelemetry project.
